🪔 Light up Diwali with your hands

Show a gesture to the camera and hold it still for a moment.

  • ☝️
    RocketPoint one finger straight up
  • ✊
    AnarMake a tight fist
  • ✋
    StarburstOpen your palm, fingers spread
  • ✌️
    Roman CandleShow a V. Tilt it to aim the shots
  • 👌
    PhooljhadiTouch thumb and index tips, then draw in the air
  • 🙏
    Grand FinalePress both palms together
  • Keep your hand in view, about an arm's length from the camera.
  • Use both hands for two crackers at once.
  • Touching your face or moving casually won't set anything off.
